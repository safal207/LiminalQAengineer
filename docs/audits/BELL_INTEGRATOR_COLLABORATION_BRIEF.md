# Bell Integrator · collaboration brief

## Why this brief exists

A bounded outside-in review of Bell Integrator's public website found several reproducible quality signals that may affect how clients and senior candidates perceive the company.

The purpose is not to assign blame or claim access to internal systems. It is to show how a small independent QA, system-analysis, and business-analysis pilot could improve public product integrity.

## Three strongest observations

### 1. QA service positioning is mapped to unrelated ABS copy

The public QA service card and its detail route use a paragraph about modernization of banking systems rather than quality assurance.

**Potential effect:** a buyer evaluating QA expertise encounters a visible content-control defect on the QA service itself.

### 2. Corporate scale and age claims are not synchronized

Different pages on the current domain show seven or nine offices/representative offices. The site also combines a fixed 20-year metric with a 2003 founding date.

**Potential effect:** clients and candidates receive inconsistent signals while assessing company maturity and operating scale.

### 3. Candidate-facing pages contain stale and defective copy

The careers and vacancy surfaces include time-relative employee stories and visible spelling, grammar, and spacing defects.

**Potential effect:** senior candidates may infer that vacancy and employer-brand content passes through weak acceptance controls.

## Proposed pilot

A compact pilot can cover:

1. the public service catalogue;
2. company and trust-building pages;
3. careers and vacancy journeys;
4. desktop/mobile consistency;
5. accessibility and keyboard-navigation basics;
6. content ownership and freshness controls.

## Deliverables

- prioritized QA findings with reproduction steps;
- system map of duplicated or conflicting content sources;
- business-impact hypotheses separated from measured facts;
- desktop/mobile evidence pack;
- remediation backlog with quick wins and structural fixes;
- a 30-minute findings walkthrough.

## Suggested pilot boundaries

- public unauthenticated pages only;
- no form submission or candidate application;
- no direct API, security, load, or intrusive testing;
- no claim about internal root cause without Bell Integrator context;
- no publication of findings without agreement.

## Suggested next step

A 15–20 minute introductory call to review the three observations, validate whether they are already known, and agree on a small pilot scope.

---

This brief is an independent outside-in assessment. It does not represent Bell Integrator, does not prove internal root cause, and does not quantify commercial loss.
